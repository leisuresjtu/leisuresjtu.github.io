Research

仿脂肪细胞准零刚度隔振器设计
受脂肪细胞微结构启发，设计准零刚度隔振器， 使用 SolidWorks 建模，并基于 Abaqus 进行有限元分析， 研究其静态与动力学隔振性能。

Bio-inspired Quasi-Zero Stiffness Vibration Isolator
This project focuses on the design of quasi-zero stiffness vibration isolators inspired by adipose cell microstructures. SolidWorks is used for modeling, and Abaqus for finite element simulations.
