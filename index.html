仿脂肪细胞准零刚度隔振器设计

Bio-inspired Quasi-Zero Stiffness Vibration Isolator

受脂肪细胞微结构启发，设计准零刚度隔振器。 使用 SolidWorks 进行结构建模，基于 Abaqus 开展有限元仿真分析，研究结构的静态与动力学隔振性能。

Inspired by adipose cell microstructures, this project focuses on the design of quasi-zero stiffness vibration isolators. SolidWorks is used for modeling, and Abaqus is employed for finite element simulations.
